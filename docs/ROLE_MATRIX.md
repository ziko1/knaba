# Role and scope matrix

The source of default grants is [permissions.ts](../packages/domain/permissions.ts). The command executor checks **exact permission strings** using `includes`, never `*` or prefix wildcards. It reloads the current user and role map inside each transaction. Explicit identity grants are bounded by assigned role defaults. OWNER may record the narrowly enumerated `EXPLICIT_PRIVILEGED_PERMISSIONS` for another staff identity, with current high-risk MFA; customer/external roles and self-escalation are excluded. A non-owner cannot grant rights they do not hold. A role name or hidden button is not a scope override.

Canonical staff/customer roles are OWNER, DIRECTOR, OPERATIONS_MANAGER, DISPATCHER, INTERNAL_BAULEITER, TEAM_LEADER, EMPLOYEE, QUALITY_CONTROL, STOREKEEPER, PROCUREMENT, ACCOUNTANT, CLIENT, EXTERNAL_BAULEITER, BOT_ADMIN, AUDITOR and DEVELOPER_SUPPORT. GUEST and SERVICE_ACCOUNT are technical principals. FOREMAN→INTERNAL_BAULEITER, TEAM_LEAD→TEAM_LEADER and WAREHOUSE→STOREKEEPER are compatibility aliases for DEMO login only; new assignments use canonical names.

## Enforcement beyond a grant

- **Company:** transaction companyId is server-controlled; foreign references fail safely. `scope.company` is explicit and does not grant an otherwise missing command permission.
- **Site/warehouse:** assigned siteIds/warehouseIds and same-site reference checks apply to operational/inventory data. Warehouse role alone does not authorize another warehouse.
- **Own employee:** employee IDs in shift/time/travel/payout are authenticated user IDs. Workers act on their own records; scoped supervisors/accountants use specifically permitted review/financial paths. Self-approval is prohibited where separation is required.
- **Customer:** active, current verified CustomerMembership controls customer/site/capability actions. A name, address, phone channel or guessed order ID grants no membership. Revocation applies to portal, old callbacks, downloads and delivery.
- **Chat:** active channel membership and history-from interval apply to originals/translations/copies/attachments. External members cannot enter internal site channels.
- **High risk:** the [command catalogue](API_CONTRACTS.md) marks high-risk actions; PRODUCTION requires recent MFA. Actual legal approvals/evidence/effective dates and workflow approval still apply.

OWNER has company/identity/legal/bot/audit administration and operational grants; it has **no wildcard and no automatic payroll/bank/raw-route access**. DIRECTOR's operations/sales/report grants likewise do not imply `finance.*` or `location.history.read`. ACCOUNTANT manages confidential finance with separate approval/receipt rules. BOT_ADMIN configuration rights do not make the administrator a client-data, wage or GPS authority. DEVELOPER_SUPPORT currently has technical integration status and own identity maintenance; there is no hidden developer superuser or general impersonation.

The initial production owner bootstrap stores a bounded explicit permission set and relies on canonical OWNER defaults; it does not seed a demo actor. The synthetic DEMO actors deliberately receive explicit test rights needed to exercise workflows. That fixture is not the production role policy.

## Identity and revocation controls

`user.invite` returns a one-time, expiring activation token to an authorized administrator; automatic external email delivery is not implemented. Transfer it only through the company's approved identity-verification process. Activation hashes the password, requires TOTP for privileged roles and consumes the token atomically. It does not authenticate an unrelated email merely because that text was supplied elsewhere.

`user.roles` requires another user's ID, a reason, bounded roles/permissions and validated site/warehouse assignments. Self-escalation is forbidden. The last active owner cannot be removed or disabled. Role replacement revokes old assignment records and active sessions. `user.disable` also revokes devices. Password/MFA rotation revokes active sessions. Customer membership and channel removal independently revoke those narrower access paths. GPS policy/history and privacy management rights are explicit privileged grants, not automatic OWNER permissions. Temporary operational delegation is separately hashed/approved, scoped and expiring; it forbids sensitive rights and is reevaluated against the grantor at every action. PERSONAL knowledge belongs only to its subject; an actor carrying a customer/external role cannot retrieve INTERNAL knowledge despite an added staff role.

`legal_approval.record` records an actual external review's subject/reviewer/evidenceReference/scope/approvedAt/expiresAt and optional verified document reference. It requires authorized OWNER/DIRECTOR plus `legal.manage` and MFA. Recording a reference is not a system-provided legal opinion. Expired/revoked approvals do not authorize tax/templates/AI/GPS. A production legal gate cannot be satisfied with a synthetic company fixture.

Permission additions require updating this source-generated table and testing the affected positive/negative company/site/self/customer/warehouse paths. The full source and tests remain the authority if the table and a future release differ.
